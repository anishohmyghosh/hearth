Tend the Fire

Words fall as embers. Catch the ones that feed a healthy fire; let the ones that smother it fall.

Move  ← → or A / D, or drag anywhere
Read  healing words grow the fire, wounding words shrink it. Later embers all look the same, so read them.
Prompt  words that match the theme at the top count double, both ways.
Breathe  hold Space (or the button) to slow time. It drains, then refills. P pauses.
Win · lose  reach +10 to blaze into the next level. At −10 the fire goes out.
